EcoRoute AI

Last updated: September 16, 2026

These Terms of Service govern access to and use of EcoRoute AI, a web-based academic and research prototype developed to support data-driven municipal waste management through waste-generation prediction and garbage-collection route optimization.

1

Purpose and Scope

EcoRoute AI is developed as an academic and research-oriented software system. Its principal functions are to analyze relevant waste-management data, generate waste-generation predictions, and produce optimized collection routes. The research is focused on Barangay Talamban, Cebu City, and the intended users include local government units (LGUs), waste-management administrators, project evaluators, and other authorized users.

EcoRoute AI is decision-support software. It is not a substitute for official government procedures, field verification, emergency response, or professional judgment.

Predictions and Route Outputs

EcoRoute AI uses historical waste-collection records and relevant environmental and geographic parameters, including population density, area activity levels, collection frequencies, geographic parameters, and routing constraints, to support prediction and route generation.

Predictions and routes depend on the quality and completeness of the available data and model assumptions. They are not guaranteed to be accurate or complete and should be reviewed by authorized personnel.

The current research scope does not provide real-time dynamic rerouting around live traffic accidents or sudden road closures. The system relies on static distance information and historical parameters and is limited to prediction and route generation; it does not provide waste-segregation operations, recycling-facility operations, or landfill-management functions.
