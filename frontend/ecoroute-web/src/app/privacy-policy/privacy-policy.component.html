Privacy Policy

EcoRoute AI

Last updated: September 16, 2026

EcoRoute AI respects the privacy of individuals whose personal information is processed through the system. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed, why it is processed, how it is protected, how long it may be retained, and the rights available to data subjects. It is intended to align the project with the transparency, legitimate-purpose, and proportionality principles of the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and related National Privacy Commission guidance.

1

Scope

This Policy applies to users of the EcoRoute AI web application, including authorized LGU personnel, waste-management administrators, project evaluators, and other authorized users. The research itself is geographically focused on Barangay Talamban, Cebu City.

2

Information We May Collect or Process

  • Account information: full name, email address, username, and password information required for registration and authentication.
  • Account-management information needed for login, password recovery, password reset, account updates, and account deletion.
  • Waste-management data: historical collection records, collection dates, collection areas, waste measurements, collection status, and waste-type information.
  • Operational/planning data: population density, area activity levels, collection frequencies, geographic parameters, routing constraints, vehicle information, and other route-planning inputs.
  • System outputs: waste-generation predictions, optimized routes, route assignments, and related analytical results.

The research document identifies historical waste-generation data, geographic parameters and routing constraints, population density, area activity levels, and collection frequencies as important system inputs. Personal information should be collected only where necessary for account management, authentication, administration, security, or another declared purpose.

3

Information We Do Not Intend to Collect

EcoRoute AI is not designed to require sensitive personal information such as health information, religious or political affiliations, biometric information, or government-issued identification numbers for its core functions. Users should not submit such information unless a lawful and necessary purpose and appropriate safeguards have been established.

The statement in the groupmate draft that 'No personally identifiable information is gathered' should NOT be used because the current signup/account features include full name, email address, username, and password.

4

Purposes of Processing

  • Creating, authenticating, maintaining, updating, and deleting user accounts.
  • Providing password recovery and account-security functions.
  • Providing authorized access to EcoRoute AI.
  • Managing and analyzing waste-management records.
  • Supporting waste-generation prediction and route optimization.
  • Evaluating functionality, usability, and technical accuracy as part of the academic research.
  • Maintaining system security, reliability, and integrity.
  • Meeting applicable legal, institutional, or research requirements.
5

Lawful Basis and Consent

The appropriate lawful basis for each processing activity should be determined by the project's or institution's designated personal information controller. Where consent is relied upon, it should be freely given, specific, informed, and capable of withdrawal as provided by applicable law. A privacy notice does not itself replace a consent mechanism when consent is required.

6

Data Security

Information should be used only for declared and legitimate purposes and only to the extent necessary for those purposes. Access should be limited to authorized personnel and service providers who need the information for authorized functions.

Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards should be maintained, including access controls, secure credential handling, authentication, controlled database access, and reasonable measures against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction. Only safeguards actually implemented by the deployed system should be represented as implemented.

7

Sharing and Disclosure

Personal information should not be disclosed to third parties except where necessary for an authorized system function, required by law, permitted by the applicable lawful basis, or otherwise properly authorized. Where external service providers process information for the project, their access should be limited to what is necessary and governed by appropriate safeguards where required.

Academic publications or presentations should avoid unnecessary disclosure of identifiable personal information and should use aggregated, anonymized, or de-identified information where appropriate.

8

Research Data

The research uses historical waste-generation data and environmental/geographic parameters for prediction and route optimization. Where research data can be linked to an identifiable individual, it must be treated as personal information and handled accordingly. Properly anonymized data may be used for research subject to applicable institutional requirements.

9

Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, account and system operation, authorized research and evaluation, or applicable legal/institutional recordkeeping. When it is no longer required and no lawful retention obligation applies, it should be securely deleted, destroyed, anonymized, or otherwise disposed of according to documented procedures.

The research document does not establish a fixed deletion date. Therefore, the project should not promise automatic deletion immediately after the research ends unless the University formally adopts that retention schedule.

10

Data Subject Rights

Subject to applicable law and lawful limitations, data subjects may have rights to be informed about processing, access personal information, request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where consent is the basis, request erasure or blocking where warranted, and lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

11

Password and Account Security

Passwords are used for authentication and password-reset functions. Users must keep credentials confidential and report suspected unauthorized access. Password-reset links and tokens should be treated as confidential security credentials.

12

Children's Privacy

EcoRoute AI is intended for authorized users participating in the academic project and institutional use. The project should not knowingly collect children's personal information unless an appropriate lawful basis, authorization, and safeguards have been established.

13

Maps and External Services

The system uses web-based mapping and geographic services for route visualization and optimization. The research scope specifically depends on internet access for map rendering and optimization. External providers may have their own privacy practices and terms.

14

Security Incidents

If a personal-data security incident occurs, the project team should assess and handle it in accordance with the Data Privacy Act, applicable National Privacy Commission requirements, University procedures, and the project's incident-response process.

15

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the system, research activities, processing purposes, technologies, or applicable requirements change. Significant changes should be communicated as appropriate, and the current version should remain accessible through EcoRoute AI.

16

17

Acknowledgment

By using EcoRoute AI, users acknowledge that they have been provided this Privacy Policy and informed about the processing of their personal information. Where consent is required as the lawful basis for a particular processing activity, the system should obtain that consent separately and in a clear, specific, and informed manner.
